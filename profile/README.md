# UQ Drones Collaborative Research Platform

This is the software page for the **Drones Collaborative Research Platform (CRP)** at [The University of Queensland](https://www.uq.edu.au).

The Drones CRP is a leading hub for drone-based research and innovation, providing researchers, industry partners, and government agencies access to cutting-edge remote sensing, geophysics, direct sampling, and operational expertise.

👉 [Learn more about the Drones CRP](https://research.uq.edu.au/facilities/crp/drones)
